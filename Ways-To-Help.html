ADOPT / FOSTER

There are millions of orphaned or handicapped street animals and abandoned pets in India. 

The flood of orphaned pups and handicapped street animals in need of homes continues unabated, not to mention scores of so called 'pedigrees' being cruelly abandoned when they fall sick, grow old, lose favour of their 'masters' or simply go 'out-of-fashion'. 

Orphaned Pups & Kittens:
Scores are motherless street pups and kittens are rescued by the Blue Cross Of India in Chennai every month. 

Abandoned Pets:
Though abandoing a pet is illegal, many adult pet dogs and cats are anonymously abandoned. Many, often tied to posts overnight outside our Guindy shelter. These are rescued and  managed in our Abandoned Animals section.

Handicapped Street Animals:
Often animals that suffer road accident and are handicapped cannot fend for themselves on the streets. They are rescued by the Blue Cross Of India and managed in our Geriatric and Special Needs section. 

These are the animals that are in desparate need of loving homes.

VOLUNTEER

Contrary to popular belief, volunteers do not sign up because they have plenty of time. They show up because they have ‘plenty’ of heart. Do you have the heart to make time.

When we started almost six decades ago, Blue Cross Of India was entirely volunteer run. Over the years a body of over 2000 registered volunteers have been the spine of BCI, and continue to help support us in innumerable ways.

If you care, if you have the heart, come join this ever growing group - the 2000 plus beating hearts inside and the millions outside, need us - they need you. Join hands to build a compassionate world that we can share with them. 

Volunteering & Internship Opportunities:

HOST A DONATION BOX / QR POSTER

You can make a big difference helping with fundraising by hosting a donation box. If you own an establishment that has good footfall i.e. you have a lot of visitors everyday and wish to help a six decade old animal welfare organization in fundraising - you will be the right fit for our host-a-box program.

A donation box may be physical or virtual. 

Currently our Physical Donation Box program is only available in locations within Chennai. The boxes are made of acrylic and are compact taking up very little space on your counter tops, but look smart and tidy.

Our Virtual Donation Box program is available anwhere in the world. These are user printable boxes or posters that contain our donation QR code links that will take potential donors to our online donation page.

Who can host a 'physical' donation box OR a 'physical' QR-poster:
Office Receptions of large MNCs / Tech Companies
Individual Owned Shops (particularly Pet shops)  
Retail Chains
Malls
Airports
Railway Stations

Who can host a donation QR Code Poster:

Online:
Any personal / business website that is not ideologically conflicted with animals rights and animal welfare

USE OUR OUT-PATIENT SERVICES

The Blue Cross Of India Out Patient clinic is open to all pet owners from 9am to 9pm all 365 days of the year. An experienced veterinarian and support nursing staff man our clinic.

A donation of Rs 200/- or more is requested per visit. Your donation helps the BCI provide free healthcare for over 2000 street animals in our shelter, and free OP consultations to community animals brought by economically challenged caregivers (like slum dwellers and auto drivers who spend their own time and money in caring for street animals)

Help the Blue Cross Of India by patronising our OP services for all your pet's healthcare needs

Services Provided:
-Wellness Exams & Preventive Care
-OP Consultation Diagnosis, Laboratory Tests
-Vaccinations (pups, kitten, dogs and cats)
-Elective Day Surgeries

USE OUR DIAGNOSTICS SERVICES

The Blue Cross Of India Diagnostic Services are open to all pet owners from 9am to 9pm all 365 days of the year. An experienced veterinarian and support nursing staff man our diagnostics lab.

A voluntary donation corresponding to the costs of the relevant diagnostic tests is suggested. Your donation helps the BCI provide free healthcare for over 2000 street animals in our shelter, and free OP consultations and diagnostics (blood tests) to community animals brought by economically challeneged caregivers (like slum dwellers and auto drivers who spend their own time and money in caring for street animals)

Help the Blue Cross Of India by patronising our Diagnostics Services for all your pet's preventive and palliative diagnostic needs

USE OUR PET CREMATORIUM

The Blue Cross Of India has established the first dedicated Small Animal Crematorium in Tamilnadu at our Guindy location, with support from Chennai Willingdon Corporate Foundaiton. A safe and environmental friendly facility approved by the Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board. Lauded by the media as big boon to pet owners in Chennai and neighbouring areas who had no options to provide a safe and dignified send off to their departed pets.

A voluntary donation of Rs 2500/- or more to defer the costs for a cremation is suggested. Your donation helps the BCI provide free cremations to street animals in our shelter, and free or subsidised cremations to community animals brought by economically challeneged caregivers (like slum dwellers and auto drivers who spend their own time and money in caring for street animals)

Help the Blue Cross Of India by patronising our Crematorium Services while at the same time providing a dignified farewell to your beloved pets who have passed on.

Services Provided:
-Cremation of pets
-Overnight freezer
-Urn with ashes (on request)

BECOME AN ASSOCIATE LIFE MEMBER OF BCI

All donors who donate Rs.25,000 or more at a time will, in addition to the satisfaction they get of helping animals, be designated as an  Associate Life Member.

It is the privilege of Blue Cross of India to have over 1300 ALMs, many of whom have been repeat donors over the years, and several over the decades that we have been in the service of animals.

In addition to your donation being most optimally put to use - directly towards animal welfare, the membership gives you the satisfaction of being associated with a six decade old non profit who's members and volunteers have dedicated themselves to the welfare of the voiceless.

TAKE THE PLEDGE TO PROTECT ANIMALS

Take the BCI Pledge

The first step to shape our world into one that values and respects all animals is to recognise the intrinsic value of non human animals. A value that goes far beyond the one that considers them a property or even a pet. That all living things are intrinsically - for and by themselves valuable to our fragile ecosystem.

THE BCI PLEDGE
- I will speak up for animals in distress and report abuse/cruelty or neglect to the police. 
- I will lobby to improve the laws that recognise animal rights. 
- I will support law enforcement across the country in their efforts to enforce animal cruelty laws.
- I will talk to family and friends about animal welfare and encourage them to learn more.
